Introduction Traditional systems are built around user actions where a person browses, decides, and executes each step of a transaction. As agents begin to act on behalf of users, that interaction model is changing. A user may define what they want once, while an agent interprets those instructions, evaluates changing conditions, and ultimately executes a purchase on the user’s behalf.
